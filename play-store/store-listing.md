## Full description (≤ 4000 chars) — copy-paste below

```
Teach Quran is the companion app for Quran teachers, tutors, and online teachers: manage your students' reading, correct and track their mistakes, and read the mushaf along with them — all from one device.

Built around the classroom:
• Create profiles for each student and manage them all from your dashboard
• Follow each student's reading with per-student reading marks
• Tap any word on the mushaf page to mark it as a mistake, so you can see exactly which words need practice
• Highlight words and verses that need work, and review them with your students
• Add written notes and record voice notes directly on any verse for homework or correction feedback
• Draw and annotate right on the mushaf page to explain pronunciation and tajweed
• Share annotated pages (with drawings, mistakes, or bookmarks) with students and parents

Read the Quran along the way:
• Authentic mushaf page mode with multiple beautiful Arabic fonts
• Continuous and verse-by-verse reading modes
• Reading marks, bookmarks, and page/surah/juz index for instant navigation
• Recite surahs from any verse with a choice of renowned reciters
• Loop verses and pages for repetition and memorization
• Word-tap playback and full surah playback from the toolbar

Made for online teaching:
• Everything your students save — bookmarks, notes, highlights, drawings, and reading marks — syncs automatically across your devices with your account, so you can correct homework from your tablet and follow up from your phone
• Night mode and adjustable text brightness for comfortable reading, even during evening sessions

Teach Quran was built to make Quran teaching simpler for tutors, classrooms, and families. No ads, no tracking, no distractions — just the Quran and your students' progress.

Note: A parent or teacher creates student profiles. Voice notes are stored on the device.
```
