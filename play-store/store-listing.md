## Full description (≤ 4000 chars) — copy-paste below

```
Teach Quran is a complete Quran reading and memorization companion for students, parents, and teachers.

Read the mushaf in authentic page mode with multiple beautiful Arabic fonts, or switch to continuous and verse-by-verse modes. Every page is tappable: tap any word to hear or study it, long-press a verse for Play, Bookmark, Reading Mark, Note, Record, and Copy.

Track your students' progress:
• Manage multiple student profiles from your dashboard
• Reading marks follow each student's last position
• Bookmark verses for review
• Highlight words and verses to show what needs work
• Add written notes and record voice notes directly on the verse
• Draw and annotate right on the mushaf page

Everything your students save — bookmarks, notes, highlights, drawings, and reading marks — syncs automatically across your devices with your account, so progress is never lost.

Audio features:
• Recite surahs from any verse with a choice of renowned reciters
• Loop verses and pages for repetition and memorization
• Word-tap playback and full surah playback from the toolbar

Study helpers:
• Mistakes tracking so you know exactly which words need practice
• Share annotated pages (with drawings, mistakes, or bookmarks) with students and parents
• Surah, juz, and page index for instant navigation
• Night mode and adjustable text brightness for comfortable reading

Teach Quran was built to make Quran memorization simpler for families and classrooms. No ads, no tracking, no distractions — just the Quran and your students' progress.

Note: A parent or teacher creates student profiles. Voice notes are stored on the device.
```
