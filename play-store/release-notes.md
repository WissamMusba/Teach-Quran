# Release Notes — v1.0.0

Copy into the release notes box (Production → Create new release) when uploading the AAB.

---

**What's new**

Read the mushaf in authentic page mode with multiple Arabic fonts, or switch to
continuous and verse-by-verse reading modes.

- Multi-student profiles — track progress for each student separately
- Sync across devices: bookmarks, notes, highlights, drawings, and reading marks
- Word-tap and verse-tap playback with multiple reciters; loop verses and pages
- Verse menu: Play, Bookmark, Reading Mark, Note, Voice Note, Copy
- Draw and annotate directly on mushaf pages
- Mistakes tracking to focus practice
- Share annotated pages with drawings, mistakes, or bookmarks
- Surah, juz, and page index navigation
- Night mode and adjustable brightness
- No ads, no tracking
