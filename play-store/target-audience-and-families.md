# Target Audience & Families Policy

## Target audience

| Age group | Target? |
|-----------|---------|
| Age 5 and under | No (app assumes reading ability) |
| Ages 6–8 | Yes (with parent/teacher supervision) |
| Ages 9–12 | Yes |
| Ages 13–15 | Yes |
| Ages 16–17 | Yes |
| Ages 18+ | Yes (parents, teachers) |

The App is **designed for use by children under 13 under the supervision of a parent or
teacher**. This makes it a **"Families" app** — the Families Policy requirements apply.

## Families policy requirements — what we already meet

| Requirement | Status |
|-------------|--------|
| No ads | ✅ App contains no ads |
| Child-directed data handling (COPPA) | ✅ Only a parent/teacher enters child data; voice notes stay on-device; no behavioral targeting |
| Prominent disclosure that the app is for families | Put this sentence in the full description |
| Privacy policy mentions children | ✅ Already included in `privacy-policy.md` §6 |
| No content inappropriate for children | ✅ Quran learning content only |

## In the console

1. **Target audience**: select "Yes, this app targets children" (ages under 13).
2. **Ads**: No.
3. **Contains ads policy (Families)**: Not applicable — no ads.
4. Under **App content → Target audience and content**, complete the short **Families**
   declaration ("This app is not designed for a specific audience that would alter its
   design based on device factors", etc. — answer truthfully; the app has no child-account
   restrictions because the parent/teacher owns the account).

> Note: Google may require the app to go through a **Families policy review** — allow extra
> time for the first review.
